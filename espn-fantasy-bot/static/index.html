    How do I find my cookies?
    1. On a computer, log in at fantasy.espn.com.
    2. Press F12 (Mac: ⌥⌘I) to open developer tools.
    3. Chrome/Edge: Application tab → Cookies. Firefox: Storage → Cookies.
    4. Click https://fantasy.espn.com and copy the values of espn_s2 and SWID.
    These act like your password. They're saved only on this computer, in the .env file.
